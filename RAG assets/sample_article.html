Understanding Retrieval-Augmented Generation

By Practice Author

2026-09-23

RAG lets an application retrieve relevant information before asking a language model to generate an answer.

The retrieval stage searches a collection of chunks. The generation stage receives the question and selected context.

Why retrieval matters

A language model can only use information available in its context. Retrieval gives the application a way to supply external knowledge.
